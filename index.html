Metrology & Rigorous Inspection

Every batch leaving CCPL undergoes stringent dimensional and metallurgical verification backed by traceable calibration certificates.

01

Incoming Raw Material QC

Optical emission spectroscopy for chemical composition analysis, ultrasonic testing for internal flaws, and hardness testing (Brinell/Rockwell) on all steel bars and SG iron castings.

02

In-Process Statistical QC

Operators perform hourly sample inspections using digital micrometers, bore gauges, and air gauges with SPC chart logging to prevent tool wear deviations.

03

Final Dispatch Audit

Final lot inspection in our temperature-controlled Mitutoyo metrology lab issuing complete Inspection Test Certificates (ITC) with every dispatch consignment.
